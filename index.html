NEXORA Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Applies to NEXORA for Android, version 0.6.0 and later

NEXORA is designed to work on your phone without an account and without internet. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored only on your phone

NEXORA stores the following on your device only. We (the developer) do not receive it.

  • Notes, reminders, trips and checklists, in the app's local database.
  • Emergency contacts and medical details, in Android's secure storage.
  • Battery readings, kept on the device for battery history.
  • Messaging contacts, their public keys and message text. Your private encryption key is kept in Android's secure storage.
  • Settings such as your theme.

Android app backup is turned off for NEXORA, so this data is not copied to Google Drive backups. Uninstalling the app or using Settings > Delete all my data removes it.

Data you choose to send

  • SOS messages. When you send an SOS, NEXORA opens your SMS, phone or sharing app with a message you can review. What you send, and to whom, is handled by that app and your mobile network.
  • Location. NEXORA reads your location only when you tap to add it to an SOS message. It never tracks your location in the background.
  • Export. Settings > Export my data saves a readable file wherever you choose. It is not encrypted. Keep it safe.

Google ML Kit (QR scanning)

QR codes are read on your phone by Google ML Kit. Camera images and scan results are not sent to Google. Under Google's terms, ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage information to Google, such as device and app information, performance metrics, error codes and a per-installation identifier. Google uses this to maintain and improve ML Kit. See Google's ML Kit terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not run servers that receive your data, and you do not need an account.
  • We do not show ads, use advertising IDs, or sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.

Children

NEXORA is not directed at children under 18.

Changes

If NEXORA adds optional accounts, cloud backup or subscriptions, this policy will be updated before those features are released. It will explain what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.
